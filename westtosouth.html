Motion & Graphic Design for West To South Club

About the project

This project was created for the West To South music club in the Y2K 2000s style. The main goal is to develop a complete package of graphics and animations that reflects the atmosphere of the early 2000s, combining the aesthetics of the club scene of that time with modern motion technologies.

Work with @underinfluence.jkt, @delulu.jkt
